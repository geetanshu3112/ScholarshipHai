      About ScholarshipHai

      ScholarshipHai is a student-focused platform that makes scholarship discovery simple. We bring scholarship information, eligibility criteria, deadlines, categories, application details, and official application links together in one place — so students can spend less time searching and more time applying.

      Privacy Policy

      ScholarshipHai is designed to help students discover scholarship opportunities. We do not require users to create an account or provide personal information to browse scholarship information on the website.

      Scholarship information displayed on ScholarshipHai may include scholarship names, eligibility criteria, deadlines, benefits, categories, and links to official application portals.

      When you click an application link, you may be redirected to a third-party website. Those websites have their own privacy policies and terms, and ScholarshipHai is not responsible for their data practices.

      We may use basic technical information or analytics, if enabled in the future, to understand website usage and improve the platform.
